# InternEdge

> **AI-Powered Internship Readiness & Career Acceleration Platform**

## Vision

InternEdge aims to bridge the gap between college education and industry
expectations by providing students with a single platform to prepare for
internships and placements. Instead of simply listing internship
opportunities, InternEdge acts as an intelligent career companion that
evaluates a student's profile, identifies gaps, recommends improvements,
and guides them from learning to placement.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Problem Statement

Students often struggle with:

-   Finding internships relevant to their skills.
-   Understanding what companies expect.
-   Tracking multiple applications.
-   Building ATS-friendly resumes.
-   Preparing for interviews.
-   Identifying skill gaps.
-   Following a structured learning path.
-   Managing everything across multiple platforms.

Current solutions solve only one part of the journey. InternEdge
combines the complete workflow into one ecosystem.

# Core Philosophy

InternEdge revolves around a **Unified Student Profile**. Every feature
reads from and contributes to this profile, allowing recommendations to
improve continuously over time.

The profile contains:

-   Education
-   Skills
-   Projects
-   Resume
-   Experience
-   Career goals
-   Learning progress
-   Applications
-   Interview performance
-   Activity history
